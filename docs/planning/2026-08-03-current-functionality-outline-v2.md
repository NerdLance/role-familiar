## Purpose and evidence standard

This snapshot records repository-supported product behavior after the role-grounded Ask Your Familiar milestone. It preserves the broader v1 inventory while updating the coaching surface, its security boundaries, and its verified test evidence.

Classification rules:

- **Working:** reachable implementation with domain and authorization behavior, normally covered by automated tests.
- **Prototype:** functional but governed by explicitly temporary product rules or presentation.
- **Foundation:** installed or rendered, but not yet a meaningful product outcome.
- **Deferred:** deliberately absent and not a defect.

## Current non-negotiable invariants

1. Queue Wyrm remains the only familiar species.
2. A user may own multiple target roles, while each role keeps isolated familiar and progression history.
3. At most one confirmed role is active; switching archives rather than overwrites history.
4. Only the authenticated user's server-resolved active role and its owned familiar may enter coaching.
5. `ConfirmedRoleContext` is the only target-role payload supplied to the coaching agent.
6. The coaching prompt contains only serialized confirmed role context and the current validated question, in addition to system-owned instructions and schema.
7. Raw listings, raw analysis output, evidence, assumptions, attempts, prompt/provider metadata, archived roles, soft-deleted content, and other users' data never enter coaching.
8. The coaching agent has no tools, browsing, or persistent conversation memory.
9. Structured coaching output remains bounded and enum-controlled; invalid output is never partially trusted or rendered.
10. User and role text are data and cannot grant tools, reveal private context, change schema, or authorize application writes.
11. Coaching cannot complete or create quests, evolve familiars, change stages or roles, award progress/XP/points, calculate readiness, or persist scores.
12. Questions, context, prompts, generated answers, provider bodies, and raw exceptions are not logged or exposed.

## Recommended delivery sequence

### Next milestone — persistent bounded practice-session contract

Define a separate product and domain contract before implementing any persistent practice session. The contract should specify session ownership, explicit start/end boundaries, retained artifacts, deletion and retention behavior, authorization inheritance, bounded AI context, user review, and what—if anything—constitutes completion evidence.

This recommendation does **not** implement or approve chat history, STAR story persistence, readiness scoring, mock interviews, competency tracking, AI-created quests, AI-completed quests, or automatic progression. Those decisions remain deferred until individually contracted and reviewed.

### Following quality milestone — product entry and browser confidence

1. Replace the placeholder Dashboard with an active-role launchpad.
2. Gate Familiar Lab outside development.
3. Add real-browser checks for 320 px layouts, keyboard behavior, async announcements, retry, reduced motion, and familiar transitions.
4. Publish a privacy-safe operations and retention runbook.
